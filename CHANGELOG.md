- **feat(discovery)**: Greenhouse board discovery and enrichment (ADR-0021).
  - Common Crawl CDX Discovery (`backend/src/discovery/CdxWorker.ts`): Implemented paged CDX query (`showNumPages=true` with page iteration and prefix fallback on 504) targeting `boards.greenhouse.io/*`, `job-boards.greenhouse.io/*`, `boards.eu.greenhouse.io/*`, and `job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io/*`.
