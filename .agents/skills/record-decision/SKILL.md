description: "Write an ADR for a decision made in this change."
---

1. Inspect `docs/decisions/README.md` to determine the next sequential ADR number `ADR-<nnnn>`.
2. Copy `docs/decisions/ADR-0000-template.md` to `docs/decisions/ADR-<nnnn>-<kebab-title>.md`.
3. Fill in Status, Date, Deciders, Related IDs (D-xx, BF-xxx, GAP-xxx), Context, Options Considered, Decision, and Consequences.
4. Update the index table in `docs/decisions/README.md`.
5. Stage the new ADR and updated index for inclusion in the commit.
